FAQ

Short answers to the usual questions.

How do I enter the leaderboard?

Create a new account on gamba.com and enter OBL in the code field at sign-up. Once your account is linked to the code, your wagers count toward the leaderboard.

I already have a Gamba account. Can I still join?

Eligibility depends on Gamba's rules for adding a code to an existing account. Check the terms on gamba.com or ask in the Discord before you play.

How is the leaderboard ranked?

Players are ranked by total amount wagered during the period shown on the leaderboard page. The timer on this page counts down to the end of the current period.

When and how are prizes paid?

Prizes are paid to the winners' Gamba accounts after each period closes and the results are verified. Announcements go out in the Discord.

Why are usernames partly hidden?

Names are masked to protect players' privacy. You will always see your own name in full on Gamba.

Does using the code cost me anything?

No. The code does not change the price of anything on Gamba. It links your account to this page so your wagers are tracked.

Is gambling risky?

Yes. You can lose money, and you should only play with what you can afford to lose. Set limits, take breaks, and stop if it stops being fun. Help is listed at the bottom of this page.

18+ Gamble responsibly

You must be 18 or older, or the legal gambling age where you live. Gambling can be addictive. If it is causing you harm, free support is available at begambleaware.org and gamblingtherapy.org.
